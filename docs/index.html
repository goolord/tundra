Tundra

A fast sample browser for your audio library.

Download

Features

Browse
Browse folders of FLAC, WAV, MP3, OGG, and AIFF samples, with fuzzy file search and directory caching.
Search by tag
Filter with title:value, artist:value, and more, with autocomplete.
Auto-tag
Tag untagged files by instrument type, or scan a whole folder, review suggestions, and apply in batch.
Preview
See waveforms and play samples back; zoom in to individual sample points.
Safe writes
Tags are written to a copy that is verified and swapped in atomically, so your audio is never left half-written.
Cross-platform
Builds for Windows, macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), and Linux.
